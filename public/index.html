Recording rules & historical evidence

Take-off: recent ground observation at this airfield, followed by two distinct reports at ≥38 km/h and ≥30 m above the ground baseline, with the first flying report inside the local boundary and below 350 m relative height. The confirming report may be higher or nearby outside the boundary. Landing: two local ground reports at ≤25 km/h, at least 15 seconds apart; an explicit ground flag or a plausible height ≤35 m is required. Missing signals never confirm landing.

Minute sampling brackets event times; it cannot give exact launch or touchdown times. Completed air time excludes partial and unresolved flights. Week, month and year totals use saved flight summaries; position tracks retain seven days.

Server tracks are collected every minute with the browser closed and retained for seven days. Download the available archive with “Server tracks”. Collection starts when enabled; earlier data is not backfilled. History: choose a recorded day, or import an ADS-B.lol / readsb aircraft trace JSON. Imports stay in this browser and are included in daily totals and backups. Historical archive · Passing aircraft remain on the live map and are excluded from the logbook.

Probable gliders are candidates, not confirmed launches. Use Edit → Aircraft classification to confirm or reject; shared changes require your board edit key.

Automatic feeds: Open Glider Network and ADS-B.lol. Glide and Seek uses the glider network. Flightradar24 and UKAFG are reference links, not connected feeds.
